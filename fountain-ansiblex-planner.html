Reading the change

What these two dev posts actually do to a Fountain alliance.

Capital placement is now the whole game

Run the same four-hop spine across Fountain twice. From a central capital it costs a battleship 66 TJ. From Y-2ANO on the Delve border it costs 280.5 TJ — the identical route, 4.25× the toll, because zone is measured from your capital, not from the bridge.

and the capital swap cooldown is now 90 days

Fountain is a cheap region to hold

The region’s widest internal span is 16.72 LY, so a well-placed capital puts every system in Zone 1–3. From C-N4OD, 50 systems are free, 55 sit at 2× and only 10 reach 6×. Zones 4 and 5 are a tax on leaving home, not on living there.

Zone 4–5 begins past 15 LY — outside Fountain entirely

Burst and sustain are different numbers

A full gate holds 1250 TJ of burst. Held at a quarter charge, where EVE’s recharge curve peaks, it regenerates about 500 TJ/hour indefinitely — against roughly 200 TJ/hour averaged over a full refill. A gate kept topped up is a slow gate.

peak regen sits at 312.5 TJ, a quarter of capacity

A doctrine fleet no longer fits through one gate

The 80-ship fleet loaded below — 40 battleships with logistics and support — draws 1,968 TJ crossing a Zone 2 bridge. The gate only holds 1,250. Roughly 50 of those hulls get through on a full charge; at Zone 3 it collapses to about 17.

the rest wait on a six-hour refill

Bridges are alliance property now

Access control drops to alliance-only, so coalition partners, renters and blue neighbours lose the network they were flying on. Bridges also carry subcapitals only. Coalitions that shared one spine across several alliances have to build and fuel their own.

liquid ozone and toll collection are gone with it

Capitals lost the network

Capitals and supercapitals can no longer traverse an Ansiblex at all; only the Rorqual keeps a pass. With anchorable cyno jammers cut from three per system to one, capital movement goes back to jump drives, cynos and fatigue.

liquid ozone and bridge tolls are removed

Method and caveats

Geometry is real. All 115 Fountain system positions come from CCP’s ESI universe endpoints and distances are true three-dimensional separations, converted at 9.4605284×1015 m per light year.

The recharge curve is modelled. CCP stated 1250 TJ capacity, a ~6.25 hour refill from empty and “the same non-linear recharge model as ship capacitor in EVE”. This page applies EVE’s standard capacitor equation with that 6.25 hour constant; the resulting average of 200 TJ/hour matches CCP’s stated figure, but the exact in-game curve has not been measured.

One row in CCP’s table does not follow its own rule. Every hull is base × multiplier except the Rorqual, published at 57.00 in Zone 2 and 104.50 in Zone 3 where base × multiplier gives 38.00 and 114.00. The table below reproduces the published numbers; treat that row as provisional.

Not modelled here. Jump drives, fatigue, isotopes and out-of-region projection into Delve, Cloud Ring, Outer Ring or Aridia. Say the word and they go in.
